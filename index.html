Frequently asked questions

What is a Title Register?+

It is the official record of a registered title, including ownership, tenure and other key entries.

What does a Title Plan show?+

It shows the general boundaries and location of the registered land. It does not normally establish a precise boundary line.

Can I search a property I do not own?+

Yes. The land register is public, so you can request copies for a property you do not own.

What if the land has no address?+

Use Map Search for fields, tracks and other land that cannot be found with a normal postal address.

How will I receive my documents?+

We send digital copies by email after processing your request.

Is Manchester Registry part of HM Land Registry?+

No. Manchester Registry is a commercial company and is not owned by or affiliated with HM Land Registry or the Government.
